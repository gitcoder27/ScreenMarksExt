Where SceneMarks Runs

Switched off on these sites and their subdomains

    On a switched-off site SceneMarks leaves the page alone: no page hotkeys, overlay or saving. Jumps from the library still work. The popup's "Turn off here" button adds the site you are on. These changes apply right away. The browser keeps the Ctrl/Cmd+Shift shortcuts on every site; clear them from the browser shortcut settings below if they clash with a site.

    Generic Pages

    SceneMarks requests all-site access and automatically detects HTML5 video on web pages. This setting controls saving on generic sites; disabling it does not change browser permissions.

    Page Hotkeys

    These hotkeys work while a supported page is focused and can be changed here. Click Record and press any key — a single letter like S works, no modifier needed. Single-key hotkeys pause while you type in text fields. Browser-level command shortcuts can also be changed from the Chromium extensions shortcuts page.

    Voice Commands

    Say a word to jump between saved timestamps, pause, play, save a moment or move through a lineup of videos. Recognition runs on this computer against your own recordings: no AI model, no network. While it is on, the browser's microphone indicator stays lit. Turn it on or off here, from the toolbar popup, or with the "Turn voice commands on or off" browser shortcut (Alt+Shift+V by default).

    1. Microphone

    On macOS, the browser also needs microphone access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

    2. Record your words

    Record each word 3 times, the way you will say it while watching. Any word in any language works; short words that sound clearly different (like "skip" and "next") are told apart best. You only need the commands you will use.

      3. Test

      Say your words and see what SceneMarks hears. Nothing runs while the test is on.
